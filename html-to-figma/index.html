    What carries over
    • Auto layout: flexbox, grids and stacked content become auto layout frames with gaps, padding, alignment, wrap and Fill/Hug sizing. Uneven spacing becomes nested stacks so every gap stays exact. Frames are named after their HTML elements.
    • Backgrounds, linear and radial gradients, borders, corner radius and drop shadows.
    • Text as live text layers with font, size, weight, colour and letter spacing.
    • Images, inline SVG icons, opacity and overflow clipping.
    • ::before/::after content, list bullets, rotated and scaled elements, inner shadows, text shadows, tiled backgrounds and icon fonts.
    • Fit content sizes the frame to the whole page, so canvases and artboards laid out beyond the screen come across in full.
    • Scroll-triggered animations are played to their end state, so content that fades in on scroll is included.
    • Not carried over: blend modes, conic gradients and video playback.
    • Auto layout pastes use Figma's own clipboard format through the open-source @figit/dom-to-figma library. Figma doesn't document that format, so if a paste ever comes out wrong after a Figma update, use "Flat layers".
    • Stylesheets, scripts and images hosted on other websites may be blocked here. The tool lists anything that didn't load; upload those files together with your HTML.
